# AI Client Hunter

**AI prospecting skills for evidence-based client outreach.**

Find a reason to contact a business before writing the first email. AI Client Hunter is a PerceptCore initiative for agencies that want to turn researched business opportunities into relevant outreach.

**Status: repository foundation.** This repository currently contains project documentation and contribution templates. Executable skills, research scripts, contact discovery, model integrations, and email delivery are not implemented. There is no package to install or runnable agent yet.

## Who it is for

SEO, marketing, and AI visibility agencies researching prospective clients. The planned AI visibility workflow focuses on evidence about how AI describes and recommends a business.

## Planned workflow

1. **Find businesses.** Start with an explicit ideal customer profile, market, and offer.
2. **Research the opportunity.** Examine public business information and, where available, recorded AI answers. Keep the original wording, source URL, and collection date behind each finding.
3. **Find the right contact.** Identify the responsible role and public business contact information. Record the source and verification status; distinguish a published address from an inferred address.
4. **Draft relevant outreach.** Turn a supported finding into a concise email and follow-up for human review. Explain why the finding matters and propose a specific next step.

A missing brand mention in one answer is a single observation. It does not establish that the business is invisible across AI systems. A discovered email address does not establish deliverability.

## Planned outputs

- A qualified prospect list with reasons for inclusion.
- An account brief separating observations, interpretations, and unknowns.
- Contact records with source URLs and verification status.
- Draft outreach tied to evidence rather than invented personalization.

## Contribute

Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Propose a concrete agency use case, an evidence format, or a research workflow. Read [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) before reporting sensitive findings. Keep prospect lists, credentials, and private client material out of public issues and pull requests.
