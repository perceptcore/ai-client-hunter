# Find a business reason to reach out

Translate the ICP into inclusion/exclusion criteria and the offer into problems the user can actually help solve. A company that exists in the right industry is a candidate, not automatically a qualified opportunity.

Search by market, category, buyer role, and relevant signals. Prefer the business's own product, pricing, about, and contact pages for company facts; use named external sources for corroboration or discovery. Follow the best relevant sources rather than generating a large query grid. Do not bypass login walls or anti-bot controls.

For each account, record:

- What the business sells, who it serves, and which ICP criteria are supported.
- A specific observation relevant to the offer, with its URL and collection date.
- Why that observation supports an opportunity; distinguish this interpretation from the observed fact.
- Any contradiction, missing evidence, or exclusion reason.

Use `qualified` only with a completed research decision and supporting evidence. Use `review` when fit or the reason to approach is unresolved. Use `excluded` for a known mismatch. Complete a reviewed/excluded account with a reason so resuming does not waste the same work. Do not infer budget, purchase intent, poor performance, or an AI visibility problem from generic website copy.

Use the domain to detect duplicate businesses, then inspect aliases before combining genuinely different entities. The helper treats `www.` and URL variants as the same domain; it does not assume that unrelated domains or every subdomain belong to the same company. Conflicting duplicates require a deliberate resolution.

If the requested count exceeds the evidence available, return fewer accounts with the remaining goal explicit. Keep private research and screenshots in the campaign directory, outside this skill's public source.
